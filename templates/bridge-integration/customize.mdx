## Why there's nothing to customize yet

The bridge integration template is planned and not published. This page will list its settings and extension points once it exists.

## Do it today

Start from [Bridge out](/guides/bridge/bridge-out) and make these choices in your own code:

- **Check before every transfer.** Read the limits and minimum fee from the contract each time, as in [Limits and fees](/guides/bridge/limits-and-fees). Don't hard-code them.
- **Show progress.** Use [Tracking transfers](/guides/bridge/tracking-transfers) to show users where a transfer is and what a refund means.
- **Get test USDT.** There's no USDT faucet. Swap tBOT for USDT with the [DEX integration template](/templates/dex-integration/index).
- **Keep amounts exact.** Convert with each token's decimals, never `parseEther`. See [USDT and decimals](/guides/defi/tokens/usdt-and-decimals).

<Warning>
Bridge transfers on mainnet move real USDT, and if a transfer is refunded the bridge fee isn't returned. Rehearse every step on testnet first.
</Warning>
