## What it will do

The bridge integration template is listed as **Planned** in [uzolabs/templates](https://github.com/uzolabs/templates). Its aim is a starter project for sending USDT out of BOT Chain through the bridge. There's no code, scope or release date yet, so this page doesn't describe files, scripts or commands. They'll appear here once the template is published.

## Do it today

Everything the template is meant to wrap already works by hand:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Learn how the bridge works">
    Read [Bridge overview](/guides/bridge/overview) for lock and release, the contracts, and why decimals change between chains.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check limits and fees">
    Follow [Limits and fees](/guides/bridge/limits-and-fees) to read the live limits and the minimum fee from the contract.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a transfer">
    Follow [Bridge out](/guides/bridge/bridge-out) to approve the bridge and call `deposit`. Rehearse on testnet first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track it">
    Follow [Tracking transfers](/guides/bridge/tracking-transfers) to follow a transfer to arrival and understand refunds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it will relate to the SDK

The bridge functions in `@uzolabs/sdk` are also planned. See [SDK bridge](/sdk/bridge/overview) for what's planned and its own do-it-today steps.
