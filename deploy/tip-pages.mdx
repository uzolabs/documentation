Share a tip page that takes USDT or BOT, and learn what your tippers see. Tips go directly to the recipient's wallet. Uzo never holds them.

## For recipients

### Create a tip jar

Deploy the [Tip Jar template](/deploy/templates/tip-jar). Set a title, such as `Coffee for Ada`, and the recipient address that should receive every tip. The recipient can't be changed later.

### Share the link

Your tip page is at `{{deploy-url}}/tip/` followed by your jar's address. The last step of the deploy shows the link with a copy button.

For more ways to share, open the jar's [manage page](/deploy/manage#tip-jar). It has:

- a QR code people can scan with their phone,
- a copy button for the tip link,
- **Open tip page**, to see what tippers see,
- a **Share** button, where your browser supports sharing.

{/* screenshot: the tip jar manage page showing the QR code and the copy link button */}

### See tips received

The manage page also shows the tips you've received: the total, the number of tips and tipping addresses, and recent tips with their messages. It updates every 30 seconds, and a new tip can take a minute to appear.

Tips arrive in your wallet at once, in the same transaction. The list is only a record.

## For tippers

### What you need

- A wallet that can connect to BOT Chain. See [Connect a wallet](/get-started/bot-chain/connect-wallet).
- USDT or BOT on the same network as the jar, plus a little BOT for gas. On testnet, both come free from the [faucet](https://faucet.botchain.ai/basic).

The tip page says when a jar is on testnet. Test coins have no value.

### Send a tip

1. Open the tip link you were given.
2. Check the recipient address shown under the title (see the safety note below).
3. Under **Pay with**, choose **USDT** or **BOT**.
4. Pick 1, 5 or 10, or enter your own amount.
5. Add a message if you like. It's optional and public, up to 140 characters (bytes), and stored on chain for anyone to read.
6. Connect your wallet. If it's on the wrong network, select **Switch to** the network shown.
7. Send the tip:
   - **BOT:** select **Send** (the button shows the amount) and approve one transaction.
   - **USDT:** select **Step 1 of 2: allow** and approve. This lets the jar move exactly this amount, nothing more. Then select **Step 2 of 2: send** and approve again.
8. When it's done, the page shows **Tip sent** and a link to the transaction.

{/* screenshot: the tip page on a phone with USDT selected and the 5 amount chosen */}

<Warning>
  **Check the recipient.** The tip page shows the address every tip goes to. Before you send, make sure it's the person you mean to tip. Anyone can deploy a tip jar with any title, so trust the address, not the title.
</Warning>

If a link doesn't open a tip page and shows "This is not a Uzo tip jar", the address wasn't created by the Uzo tip jar factory. Don't send anything to it.
