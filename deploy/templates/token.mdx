Deploy a token contract with a fixed supply, then add it to your wallet or burn some of it. For a step-by-step first deploy, follow the [Quickstart](/deploy/quickstart).

## What you get

- An **ERC-20** token with **18 decimals**.
- **ERC20Permit**: holders can approve spending with a signature instead of a transaction.
- **ERC20Burnable**: any holder can destroy tokens from their own balance.
- A **fixed supply**. The whole supply is created when the contract is deployed and sent to one address. There is no mint function after that.
- **No owner**. Nobody, including you, can mint more, pause transfers or change the token.

The contract never holds funds.

## Form fields

| Field | What it means | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Token name | The name shown in wallets and on BOTScan. | 1 to 64 characters. |
| Symbol | The short ticker, such as `MTK`. | 1 to 11 characters, letters and numbers only. |
| Total supply | How many tokens to create, in whole tokens. The app adds the 18 decimals for you. | A whole number of at least 1. Commas, spaces and underscores are ignored. Defaults to 1,000,000. |
| Send the supply to | The address that receives every token. | A valid address, not the zero address. Starts as your connected wallet. |

The supply can't be changed after deployment, so check the number on the **Review** step.

## After you deploy

- **Add to wallet.** The final step opens your wallet's "add token" prompt. If you dismissed it, select **Add to wallet** again, or import the token by hand with its address, symbol and 18 decimals.
- **Burn.** On the token's [Manage page](/deploy/manage#token), you can burn tokens from your own balance. Burning lowers the total supply and can't be undone.
- **View on BOTScan.** The contract is verified automatically. See [Verification](/deploy/verification).

## What it does not do

Uzo Deploy deploys a token contract. That's all. It does not:

- add liquidity or create a trading pool,
- put the token on any exchange or list,
- promote the token, or give it any value.

A token deployed with Uzo Deploy is not reviewed or endorsed by Uzo Labs. See [Security](/deploy/security).

## Source and customizing

The contract is `UzoToken.sol` in the [uzo-deploy repo](https://github.com/uzolabs/uzo-deploy/tree/main/contracts/src/templates), built on OpenZeppelin Contracts.

To change how the token works (for example an owner who can mint), use the templates repo instead. See the [token walkthrough](/templates/token/walkthrough).
