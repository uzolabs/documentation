Deploy a tip jar and get a link people can use to tip you in USDT or BOT, with a short message. To share it and see tips, read [Tip pages](/deploy/tip-pages).

## What it does

- Every tip goes **directly to the recipient in the same transaction**. The contract never holds a balance.
- Tips can be in **USDT** or **BOT** (tBOT on testnet).
- Each tip can carry a public **message** of up to 140 bytes. It's stored on chain for anyone to read.
- The jar has **no owner**. Nobody, including you, can change the title or the recipient after deployment.
- Plain transfers to the jar's address are rejected. Tips have to go through the tip page, or a call to `tipNative` or `tipToken`.

## Form fields

| Field | What it means | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title (optional) | The name shown on your tip page, such as `Coffee for Ada`. | Up to 64 bytes. Most letters are 1 byte each. Some characters, such as emoji, use more. |
| Recipient | The address every tip is forwarded to. | A valid address, not the zero address. Starts as your connected wallet. Can't be changed later. |

Check the recipient on the **Review** step. If it's wrong, deploy a new jar.

## How a USDT tip works

USDT on BOT Chain doesn't support signature approvals (permit), so a USDT tip takes two transactions:

1. **Allow.** The tipper approves the jar to move exactly the tip amount. The app never asks for an open-ended approval.
2. **Tip.** The jar moves the USDT from the tipper to the recipient.

A BOT tip takes one transaction.

USDT has 6 decimals, not 18. The tip page handles this for you. If you read tip amounts yourself, see [USDT and decimals](/guides/defi/tokens/usdt-and-decimals).

Testnet USDT comes from the [faucet](https://faucet.botchain.ai/basic). See [Get testnet tokens](/get-started/bot-chain/get-testnet-tokens).

## After you deploy

The final step shows your tip link with a copy button. Open the jar's [Manage page](/deploy/manage#tip-jar) for a QR code and the tips you've received.

## Source

The contract is `UzoTipJar.sol` in the [uzo-deploy repo](https://github.com/uzolabs/uzo-deploy/tree/main/contracts/src/templates), built on OpenZeppelin Contracts.
