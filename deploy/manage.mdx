Use the manage page to see a contract's details and take the actions its template allows.

## Open the manage page

There are three ways in:

- Select **Manage** on the last step of a deploy.
- Open [My contracts](/deploy/my-deployments) and select a contract.
- Go to `{{deploy-url}}/manage/` followed by the contract address.

The page only works for contracts created by a [Uzo factory](/deploy/contracts). For any other address it shows "Not a Uzo template".

Anyone can open a manage page and read it. Actions that change the contract need the right wallet, as listed below.

## Token

The token page shows the name, symbol, total supply, your balance, the contract address and the decimals (18). It also says the token has no owner: nobody can mint more, pause it or change it.

| Action | Who can do it | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add to wallet | Anyone | Opens your wallet's "add token" prompt. |
| Burn | Any holder, from their own balance | Destroys tokens and lowers the total supply. |

<Warning>
  Burning can't be undone. The tokens are gone for good.
</Warning>

## NFT Collection

The collection page shows the name, symbol, how many are minted out of the maximum, the royalty, the owner, the contract, the metadata link and whether it's frozen.

| Action | Who can do it | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mint | Owner | Mints new tokens to an address you choose. One at a time, or up to 100 per transaction. You can't go past the maximum supply. |
| Metadata link | Owner | Sets or changes the base URI. End it with a slash. Hidden once the metadata is frozen. |
| Freeze metadata | Owner | Locks the metadata link forever. You type the collection symbol to confirm. |
| Transfer ownership | Owner | Offers the collection to a new address. See below. |
| Accept ownership | The address being offered the collection | Makes that wallet the owner. |

If your wallet isn't the owner, the page says "Only the owner can mint or change settings."

<Warning>
  Freezing the metadata can't be undone. It locks the link only, not the files the link points to. See [NFT Collection](/deploy/templates/nft#freezing-is-permanent).
</Warning>

### Transfer ownership

Ownership moves in two steps, so a typo can't send your collection to an address nobody controls:

1. The owner enters the new owner's address and signs.
2. The new owner opens the same manage page with their wallet and selects **Accept ownership**.

Until the new owner accepts, you're still the owner, and you can select **Cancel pending transfer**. Once they accept, you lose every owner action.

## Tip Jar

The tip jar page shows the title, the recipient and the contract. It also says the jar has no owner: tips go straight to the recipient, and nobody can change the title or the recipient.

| Section | Who can see it | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Share | Anyone | Shows a QR code, a copy button for the tip link, **Open tip page**, and a **Share** button on phones. |
| Tips received | Anyone | Shows totals, the number of tips and tippers, and recent tips with their messages. |

Tips received updates every 30 seconds. A new tip can take a minute to appear. There are no owner actions, because the jar has no owner. See [Tip pages](/deploy/tip-pages).
