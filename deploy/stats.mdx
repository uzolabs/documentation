The stats page at [deploy.uzolabs.xyz/stats](https://deploy.uzolabs.xyz/stats) shows how Uzo Deploy is used. This page explains what it counts. For the current numbers, open the stats page itself.

## What it shows

Each network (testnet and mainnet) has its own set of numbers.

| Figure | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| Contracts deployed | Every contract created by a Uzo factory on that network. |
| Per template | Contracts deployed for each template: Tokens, NFT Collections and Tip Jars. |
| Unique deployers | Different wallets that deployed at least one contract. |
| Tips sent | Tips sent through Uzo tip jars. |
| Unique tippers | Different wallets that sent at least one tip. |
| Deployments per day | A daily chart of deployments. |
| Tips per day | A daily chart of tips. |

## Where the numbers come from

All numbers come from on-chain data: the `Deployed` events from the [Uzo factories](/deploy/contracts) and the tip events from Uzo tip jars.

Counts refresh every five minutes, and days are in UTC. Counting can take up to half a minute when the page first loads.

## Counts only

The stats page shows counts. It never lists the tokens, collections or tip jars people deployed, and it doesn't rank them. A contract deployed with Uzo Deploy isn't promoted or endorsed by Uzo Labs.

## Team activity

Contracts and tips from Uzo team wallets, such as test deployments, are left out of the counts. A line under the charts says how many deployments and tips were left out, and from how many team wallets. The team addresses are listed in `config/team-addresses.json` in the [uzo-deploy repo](https://github.com/uzolabs/uzo-deploy).
