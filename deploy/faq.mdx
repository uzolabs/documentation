Short answers to the questions people ask most about Uzo Deploy.

## Does Uzo charge fees?

No. Uzo takes nothing. You pay only the network's gas, and the app shows the estimated cost before you sign. On testnet, gas is paid in free tBOT. See [Networks and costs](/deploy/networks-and-costs).

## Can Uzo take my tokens or change my contract?

No. The factories and templates have no admin keys, no pause and no upgrade path, so nobody at Uzo can change a contract after it's deployed or move what it holds. The tip jar never holds funds at all. See [Security](/deploy/security#what-uzo-cant-do).

## Is it audited?

No. The contracts are tested, not audited. They have 86 tests, 100% coverage and static analysis, and the results are public in the repo. No independent audit has been done yet. Don't use them for large amounts until one is. See [Security](/deploy/security#what-was-checked).

## Is Uzo Deploy part of BOT Chain?

No. Uzo Deploy is an independent project by Uzo Labs, built on BOT Chain. Uzo Labs isn't affiliated with BOT Chain.

## Can I deploy something that isn't in the list?

Not in Uzo Deploy. It only deploys its own templates. To change a template or deploy your own contract, use the [templates repo](/templates/overview), or set up [Foundry](/guides/deploy/deploy-with-foundry) or [Hardhat](/guides/deploy/deploy-with-hardhat) yourself.

## Can I launch a token with liquidity here?

No. Uzo Deploy is a developer tool, not a launchpad. It creates the token contract and nothing else: no liquidity, no trading pairs and no promotion. If you want to work with liquidity yourself, see the [BDEX guides](/guides/defi/liquidity/v2).

## Which wallets work?

The app connects through a standard wallet picker that lists Rainbow, Base, MetaMask and WalletConnect. Any wallet that can add a custom EVM network should work. None has been formally tested yet, so if one doesn't work, [open an issue](https://github.com/uzolabs/uzo-deploy/issues).

## Is the code open source?

Yes. The app and the contracts are public in the [uzo-deploy repo](https://github.com/uzolabs/uzo-deploy) under the MIT license.

## Does it work on mainnet?

Not yet. The factories are deployed on BOT Chain Testnet only. See [Contracts](/deploy/contracts).
