Pick a project idea for a BOT Chain hackathon from these example tracks, each with the docs and templates that get you started fastest.

<Note>
  These tracks are **examples**. Each event sets its own tracks, rules and prizes. Check with your organizers.
</Note>

## AI agents

Build an agent that can act on-chain within limits a human sets.

**Ideas:**
- A shopping or bill-paying agent that spends from a vault with a daily cap.
- An agent that asks for human approval above a threshold.
- A treasury bot that reports balances and proposes transfers.

**Start with:** [AI agents overview](/guides/ai-agents/overview), [Agent vaults](/guides/ai-agents/agent-vaults), [Spending limits](/guides/ai-agents/spending-limits), [Human approval](/guides/ai-agents/human-approval). The [AI agent template](/templates/ai-agent/index) is in development.

## Payments

Move value between people and businesses, often in USDT.

**Ideas:**
- A checkout or invoice link that accepts testnet USDT.
- Split bills or group savings, settled on-chain.
- A gasless app where new users pay nothing to start.

**Start with:** [USDT and decimals](/guides/defi/tokens/usdt-and-decimals) (USDT has 6 decimals on BOT Chain; get testnet USDT by [swapping tBOT on BDEX V2](/guides/defi/swaps/bdex-v2)), [Gasless transactions](/guides/gasless/overview), the [Gasless app template](/templates/gasless-app/index) and the [Token template](/templates/token/index).

## DeFi

Build on BDEX, BOT Chain's Uniswap V2 and V3 style exchange.

**Ideas:**
- A swap interface with clear slippage and price impact.
- A dashboard that tracks pools and liquidity positions.
- A savings or rewards token with its own pool.

**Start with:** [Swaps overview](/guides/defi/swaps/overview), [Slippage and deadlines](/guides/defi/swaps/slippage-and-deadlines), [Liquidity on V2](/guides/defi/liquidity/v2) and the [DEX integration template](/templates/dex-integration/index).

## Open

Anything else that uses BOT Chain: NFTs, ticketing, credentials, games, community tools.

**Start with:** the [NFT template](/templates/nft/index), [Build your first dApp](/get-started/first-dapp) and [Events without getLogs](/guides/frontend/events-without-getlogs), which matters for feeds and galleries.

## Before you choose

- **Scope small.** One working flow beats five half-built features. Judges can only score what they can see.
- **Check the limits.** BOT Chain's public RPCs have no WebSockets, and `eth_getLogs` is documented as disabled. Read [Known limitations](/get-started/bot-chain/known-limitations) before you design around events.
- **Stay on testnet.** Every track can be built and demoed with tBOT and testnet USDT.
