Claim free tBOT from the BOT Chain faucet so you can pay for gas on testnet. tBOT has no value and can't be used on mainnet.

## What you'll do

Send up to 10 tBOT to your wallet address, then check that it arrived.

## Prerequisites

- A wallet address. Create one with `cast wallet new` (see the [quickstart](/get-started/quickstart)) or use MetaMask (see [Connect a wallet](/get-started/bot-chain/connect-wallet)).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the faucet">
    Go to the [BOT Chain faucet](https://faucet.botchain.ai/basic). The network should read **BOT Chain Testnet**.

    {/* screenshot: faucet.botchain.ai/basic with the network, address field and verification check visible */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your address">
    Paste your wallet address (it starts with `0x`). Double-check it; tokens sent to the wrong address can't be returned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the check and claim">
    Complete the "I am not a robot" check, then click the send button. The faucet shows a transaction hash when it's done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify it worked

Click the transaction hash the faucet returns to open it on BOTScan testnet. Or check your balance from a terminal:

```bash
cast balance --ether YOUR_ADDRESS --rpc-url https://rpc.bohr.life
```

You should see up to `10.000000000000000000`.

## Limits

Each address can claim **up to 10 tBOT every 24 hours** (checked 2026-10-01 on the faucet and in [BOT Chain's faucet guide](https://dev-docs.botchain.ai/docs/Developers/claim-test-tbot-tokens/)).

That's plenty for development. Deploying and calling a small contract costs a tiny fraction of one tBOT. See [Gas and fees](/get-started/bot-chain/gas-and-fees) for how to check costs yourself.

## When you need more

- **Wait for the next claim.** You can claim again after 24 hours.
- **Use one funded wallet for deploys.** Keep one deployer wallet and claim for it each day, rather than spreading tokens across many test addresses.
- **Test locally first.** Run your tests on a local chain with `forge test` or `anvil`, which cost nothing. Only deploy to testnet when the code works.
- **Teams and hackathons.** Ask a teammate to claim for their own address and send you some. If you're running an event, see the [faucet plan](/hackathons/organizers/faucet-plan).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The faucet won't let me claim again">
    You've probably hit the 24-hour limit for that address. Wait 24 hours, or use one of the options in [When you need more](#when-you-need-more).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The claim succeeded but my wallet shows 0">
    Check that your wallet is on **BOT Chain Testnet** (chain 968), not mainnet. Look up your address on [BOTScan testnet](https://scan.bohr.life) to confirm the balance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The verification check won't load">
    Disable ad or script blockers for the faucet page and reload.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
