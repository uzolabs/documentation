This page explains how liquidity works on BDEX V3, how it differs from V2, and where to go to manage a position.

## Concentrated liquidity

In a V2 pool, your liquidity covers every possible price. Most of it sits at prices the pool never reaches, so it earns little.

In a V3 pool, you choose a price range. Your tokens only trade while the price is inside it. The same deposit earns a larger share of fees, because it's concentrated where trading happens. The tradeoffs:

- **Out of range, you earn nothing.** If the price leaves your range, your position becomes 100% one token and stops earning until the price comes back or you move it.
- **You manage it.** A narrow range earns more but goes out of range more often.
- **Each pool has a fee tier.** BDEX V3 pools charge 0.05%, 0.30% or 1.00%, and the same pair can have a pool on each tier.

## Positions are NFTs

V3 positions aren't fungible, because each one has its own range. The NonfungiblePositionManager mints each position as an NFT. You use it to:

- `mint` a new position with a pool, a tick range and amounts of both tokens,
- `increaseLiquidity` and `decreaseLiquidity` on an existing position,
- `collect` the fees and withdrawn tokens,
- `burn` the NFT once the position is empty.

Withdrawing is two steps: `decreaseLiquidity` moves the tokens into the position's owed balance, and `collect` sends them to you.

The NonfungiblePositionManager is at `0xDAc3FcFF004d8a8675b94E44941A1a2e3b240090` on both mainnet and testnet. See [Contract addresses](/reference/contract-addresses).

## Ticks and ranges

Prices in V3 are expressed as ticks. Each pool only accepts ticks that are a multiple of its tick spacing, which depends on the fee tier. Picking a range means converting the prices you want into valid ticks for that pool. Libraries such as the Uniswap V3 SDK do this math, and BDEX V3 uses the same model.

## Manage positions

For full instructions on minting, adjusting and closing positions, see BOT Chain's [DEX documentation](https://dev-docs.botchain.ai/docs/DEX/). To list pools and check their liquidity before you choose one, use the [BDEX API](/reference/bdex-api).

## V2 or V3

| | V2 | V3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Range | All prices | One range you pick |
| Position | Fungible LP token | NFT |
| Fees | Added to the pool automatically | Collected with `collect` |
| Upkeep | None | Watch the range |

If you want to provide liquidity without watching it, start with [V2 liquidity](/guides/defi/liquidity/v2).
